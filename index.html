हनुमान चालीसा

Hanuman Chalisa by Tulsidas: all 40 verses with the two opening couplets the closing couplet and the closing cries, in Devanagari, Telugu and English sounds, with a plain meaning for each.

Listen: M. S. Subbulakshmi, Samarpanam (Saregama)
Text by Tulsidas (16th century, public domain). Meanings are plain-English renderings written for children. Devanagari and English sounds follow the printed edition supplied by the site owner. Telugu is generated from the Devanagari by rule and may differ slightly from a printed edition. Word-by-word is available for verses 7 to 16. Recording: M. S. Subbulakshmi and Radha Vishwanathan, Samarpanam, Saregama India.
