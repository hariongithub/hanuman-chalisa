हनुमान चालीसा

Hanuman Chalisa by Tulsidas: all 40 verses with the two opening couplets and the closing couplet, in Devanagari, Telugu and English sounds, with a plain meaning for each.

Listen: M. S. Subbulakshmi, Samarpanam (Saregama)
Text by Tulsidas (16th century, public domain). Meanings are plain-English renderings written for children. Telugu spellings are machine-converted from the Devanagari and may not match a printed Telugu edition exactly. Word-by-word is available for verses 7 to 16. Recording: M. S. Subbulakshmi and Radha Vishwanathan, Samarpanam, Saregama India.
