हनुमान चालीसा

Hanuman Chalisa by Tulsidas: all 40 verses with the two opening couplets the closing couplet and the closing cries, in Devanagari, Telugu and English sounds, with a plain meaning for each.

Listen: M. S. Subbulakshmi, Samarpanam (Saregama)
Text by Tulsidas (16th century, public domain). Meanings and word-by-word glosses are plain-English renderings written for children from general knowledge of Hindi, Awadhi and Sanskrit, not from a published dictionary. Glosses marked less certain are the ones most worth checking with a teacher. Devanagari and English sounds follow the printed edition supplied by the site owner. Telugu is generated from the Devanagari by rule and may differ slightly from a printed edition. Recording: M. S. Subbulakshmi and Radha Vishwanathan, Samarpanam, Saregama India.
